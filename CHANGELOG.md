# Changelog

All notable changes to Klondike: Gold Rush Solitaire are recorded here.

## [2.2.0] - 2026-11-17

- Hint button (or H): shows a useful move, and the next one each time you press it. If you're stuck it says so.
- Right-click the table (or hold a finger on it, or press C) to send every card that can go up to the foundations.
- Redo (Ctrl+Y), unlimited undo, and Restart This Deal from the New Game menu.
- Daily Claim: one winnable deal a day, the same for everyone, with a daily streak and a two-week calendar.
- Options: Vegas scoring with a running poke of gold dust, winnable deals only, a left-handed layout, four-colour suits, large cards, and touch-sized controls.
- Unlock new card backs (Yukon Night, Spruce Green, Midnight Sable) and tables (Dark Walnut, Dawson Saloon, Riverboat Blue) by winning.
- Statistics: games, wins, win rate, streaks, fastest and average wins and best score, for Draw One and Draw Three.
- Assay stamps: fourteen achievements to earn, from First Strike to Week of Gold.
- Keyboard play: arrows move, Enter picks up and puts down, Space draws.
- Touch screens: cards follow your finger straight away, taps work like clicks, and the toolbar, menus and options get finger-sized on touch PCs.
- Close Klondike mid-game and continue right where you left off next time.
- A game now counts as played once you make your first move.

## [2.1.1] - 2026-11-17

- Fixed: on smaller screens and at higher display scaling, the score, moves, time and wins ran over the toolbar buttons. The toolbar now fits any window: the subtitle steps aside first, then the buttons shrink, and on narrow windows the stats move to a row of their own.

## [2.1.0] - 2026-11-03

- New claim register: a local top-ten leaderboard of your best wins (toolbar > Scores), with time, moves, draw mode and date.
- Sign your nickname when you win to put your score on the register. Your name is remembered for next time.
- Online leaderboard coming soon.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-03

- Klondike is now a native Windows game written in Go. It no longer opens a browser window, and it starts faster.
- Same card art, table, music and sound effects as before.
- New win celebration: the whole deck flies off the foundations into a turning wheel while gold dust bursts over the table.
- Text is drawn by Windows in Georgia, as before, and stays sharp at any display scaling.
- Settings and the win record carry over from 1.x automatically.
- The window remembers its size; F11 toggles full screen.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-20

- Added the Credits & Changelog screen (toolbar > Credits).
- Added Ironite branding to the title screen and table.
- Version number now shown in the corner of the table.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

- First release of Klondike: Gold Rush Solitaire for Windows.
- Classic Klondike rules with Draw One and Draw Three.
- HD Yukon-themed deck: prospector kings, dance hall queens, Mountie jacks and Yukon Gold Co. aces.
- Weathered plank table with animated dealing and card flips.
- Four original frontier music tracks with volume control and mute.
- Drag and drop, click-to-move, unlimited undo and auto-finish.
- Windows-style scoring, timer, time bonus and saved win record.
- Bouncing-card win celebration.
