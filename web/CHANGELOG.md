# Changelog

All notable changes to Klondike: Gold Rush Solitaire are recorded here.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-20

- Added the Credits & Changelog screen (toolbar > Credits).
- Added Ironite branding to the title screen and table.
- Version number now shown in the corner of the table.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

- First release of Klondike: Gold Rush Solitaire for Windows.
- Classic Klondike rules with Draw One and Draw Three.
- HD Yukon-themed deck: prospector kings, dance hall queens, Mountie jacks and Yukon Gold Co. aces.
- Weathered plank table with animated dealing and card flips.
- Four original frontier music tracks with volume control and mute.
- Drag and drop, click-to-move, unlimited undo and auto-finish.
- Windows-style scoring, timer, time bonus and saved win record.
- Bouncing-card win celebration.
