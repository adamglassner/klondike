KLONDIKE - Gold Rush Solitaire  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
===============================

Double-click Klondike.exe to play. Nothing to install.

Klondike is a native Windows program - no browser or other software
needed. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
"More info" then "Run anyway" - the app is just not code-signed.

HOW TO PLAY
- Drag cards, or click (tap) a card to send it to the best spot.
- Click the deck to draw. Click the empty deck spot to turn the pile over.
- Build each suit from Ace to King on the four foundations.
- Right-click the table (or hold a finger on it) to send every card
  that can go up to the foundations.
- Hint shows a useful move; press it again for the next one.
- New Game's arrow menu has Restart This Deal and the Daily Claim:
  one winnable deal a day, the same for everyone.
- Win a game to sign your nickname on the claim register.
  Toolbar > Scores also has your statistics, the Daily Claim
  calendar and your assay stamps (achievements).
- Options: Vegas scoring, winnable deals only, left-handed layout,
  four-colour suits, large cards, touch-sized controls, and the
  card backs and tables you unlock by winning.
- An unfinished game is saved when you close Klondike, and you can
  continue it next time.

KEYS
- F2 new game, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H hint, C collect,
  F11 or Alt+Enter full screen.
- Play by keyboard: the arrow keys move around the table, Enter picks
  cards up and puts them down (twice on the same spot sends them to
  the best place), Space draws, Esc lets go.

Works with a touch screen too: drag with a finger, tap to move,
and the controls grow to finger size automatically.

The music, card art and table are all original.
Settings and your win record are saved automatically in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\KlondikeGoldRush.
