KLONDIKE - Gold Rush Solitaire  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
===============================

Double-click Klondike.exe to play. Nothing to install.

Klondike is a native Windows program - no browser or other software
needed. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
"More info" then "Run anyway" - the app is just not code-signed.

HOW TO PLAY
- Drag cards, or click a card to send it to the best spot.
- Click the deck to draw. Click the empty deck spot to turn the pile over.
- Build each suit from Ace to King on the four foundations.
- Win a game to sign your nickname on the claim register
  (toolbar > Scores shows the top ten).
- F2 = new game, Ctrl+Z = undo, F11 = full screen.
- Draw One / Draw Three takes effect on the next deal
  (or right away if you haven't moved yet).

The music, card art and table are all original.
Settings and your win record are saved automatically in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\KlondikeGoldRush.
